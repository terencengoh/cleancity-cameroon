# CleanCity Cameroon: operating, legal and funding plan

> Working notes, not legal advice. Every figure below comes from the linked source. Where a source could not be checked
> directly, the text says so. **Have a Cameroonian labour/social-security lawyer and the partner council review this plan before launch.**

## 1. The model in one paragraph
Each week, one zone of one city (rotating across cities and zones) is cleaned on **Saturday for 4 hours (e.g. 07:00–11:00)** by a
crew picked fairly from the people who signed up in that city. A supervisor checks workers in and out and signs them off. Each
worker who completes the shift receives **5,000 FCFA** by MTN MoMo or Orange Money.

**Confirmed defaults (admin-configurable in the app):**

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Weekly budget | **50,000 FCFA** |
| Pay per person (full 4 h shift) | **5,000 FCFA** |
| Zones per week | **1** (rotating) |
| Crew size | budget ÷ pay = **10 people** |
| Payout fees | shown separately, see §6 (≈ 75 FCFA per payout, ≈ 750 FCFA/week at 1.5 %) |

The selection engine never invites more people than the remaining budget allows.

## 2. Legal framing: paid casual workers, not "volunteers"
Because people are **paid** for supervised work, treat them as **occasional (casual) workers** under the Labour Code, not as volunteers.

- **Casual work is a recognised category.** Décret n° 93/577/PM du 15 juillet 1993:
  - occasional work lasts at most 15 days and is renewable once;
  - the employer must **declare its use of casual labour to the labour inspector**;
  - the worker is entitled to social protection;
  - continuous work for the same employer beyond 3 months stops being casual.

  Sources: https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/40875/CMR-40875.pdf · Labour Code (Loi 92/007) arts. 23 and 25: https://www.ilo.org/fr/media/22116/download

  *Our pattern (one 4-hour Saturday shift, fair rotation so the same person rarely serves back-to-back) fits the occasional-work model. Use a short written engagement per shift (generated by the app) and keep sign-off records.*
- **Minimum wage (SMIG)**, Décret n° 2023/00338/PM du 21 mars 2023:
  - 41,875 FCFA/month for state agents under the Labour Code;
  - **45,000** for the agricultural sector;
  - **60,000 FCFA/month** for other sectors.

  Décret n° 2024/0168/PM du 23 février 2024 raised only the state-agents figure (to 43,969 FCFA). Press reports state the 45,000 / 60,000 figures were unchanged.

  Sources: https://www.spm.gov.cm/site/sites/default/files/dpm_2023-00338_s.pdf · https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/56069/fr.html/salaire-minimum-interprofessionnel-garanti-les-nouveaux-baremes · https://www.spm.gov.cm/site/sites/default/files/decret_ndeg_2024_0168.pdf · https://ecofinances.net/2024/02/26/cameroun-le-gouvernement-fixe-le-smig-des-agents-de-letat-relevant-du-code-du-travail-a-43-969-fcfa-contre-41-875-fcfa-depuis-mars-2023/
- **Hours.** The legal week is 40 h outside agriculture (Labour Code art. 80): https://lex.cameroun.cc/fr/cm/code-du-travail-1992/art-80
  - *Our computation:* 60,000 FCFA ÷ ~173 h/month ≈ **346 FCFA/h**.
  - **5,000 FCFA for 4 h = 1,250 FCFA/h**, about 3.6× the hourly SMIG equivalent.
  - Partial shifts are paid pro rata (rounded down to 500 FCFA), which also stays above that floor.
- **Social security (CNPS).** Employers must register **all** workers with the CNPS, occasional workers included, within **8 days** of hiring, and pay contributions.
  - Sources: https://www.cnps.cm/fr/component/content/article/employeurs.html?Itemid=203&catid=25 · online services: https://www.cnps.cm/fr/employeurs/services-en-ligne-pour-employeurs.html
  - **Work-accident branch** contributions are paid by the employer alone: **1.75 % / 2.5 % / 5 %** of pay depending on the risk group. Sources: https://legecam.cm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/bb-dcs-12-decret-n-78283-du-10-juin-1978-fixant-le-taux-des-cotisation-de-la-branche-sociale-des-accidents-de-travail.pdf · https://www.cnps.cm/images/documentutile/decret%20fixant%20taux%20de%20cotisations%20sociales%20et%20plafonds%20des%20rmunrations_baremes.pdf
  - **Ask the CNPS** which group street cleaning falls into and how to declare a 4-hour shift. This plan does not assume the answer.
- **Data protection.** Phone numbers, ID numbers, GPS and photos are personal data. Follow Law n° 2024/017 on personal data protection: consent, purpose limitation, retention limits, and a contact for access and deletion. The app collects the ID number only optionally and keeps GPS only at check-in.

## 3. Council authorization and coordination
- **Communes are legally competent for street cleaning.**
  - Loi n° 2019/024 (Code général des collectivités territoriales décentralisées), art. 157, transfers to communes: the cleaning of communal streets, roads and public spaces, the fight against insalubrity, and the pre-collection of household waste.
  - Art. 218 puts cleaning of public ways under the mayor's municipal police powers.
  - Sources: https://www.minddevel.gov.cm/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/LOI_N%C2%B0_2019_024_DU_24_DECEMBRE_2019_PORTANT_CODE_GENERAL_DES_COLLECTIVITES.pdf · https://lex.cameroun.cc/fr/cm/298-law-no-2019-024-of-24-december-2019-bill-to-institute-the-general-code-of-regional-and-local-authorities/art-157
  - **Action:** get a written authorization or partnership agreement from each **commune d'arrondissement** (and inform the **Communauté urbaine** in Douala/Yaoundé) before cleaning a zone.
- **Waste must leave the site.** Collection is contracted out, so coordinate pick-up of bagged waste with the operator for each zone:
  - The Communauté Urbaine de Douala's 2018 tender covered collection plus street sweeping/cleaning (Lot 1: 44.6 bn FCFA; Lot 2: 23.9 bn FCFA TTC; 5 years): https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=12694&type_publication=AO
  - Yaoundé awarded HYSACAM 4 lots in Nov 2024: https://www.newsducamer.com/assainissement-hysacam-reconquiert-le-monopole-de-la-gestion-des-ordures-a-yaounde/
  - Since 2026, Genelcam operates in Douala 4e, and Thychlof Sarl in Yaoundé 3e and 6e: https://afrive.info/hysacam-perd-le-monopole-de-la-collecte-des-dechets-a-douala-apres-le-recrutement-de-genelcam/
- **Precedents that show councils are open to this:**
  - Douala's "Programme occupation jeunes" (2025) employed several hundred youths in cleaning and awareness work: https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-le-maire-de-douala-occupe-plus-de-300-jeunes-pendant-trois-mois/
  - Yaoundé Saturday cleaning operation supervised by the mayor (29 March 2025): https://actucameroun.com/2025/03/29/operation-nettoyage-a-yaounde-le-maire-supervise-lenlevement-des-ordures/
  - "Yaoundé 6 propre" with 500 youths: https://cameroun-jumelages-partenariats.cm/2025/04/12/operation-yaounde-6-propre-500-jeunes-de-la-municipalite-sassocient-a-lexecutif-municipal/
  - Douala 5e cleanliness campaign: https://douala5.cm/douala-5e-engage-une-campagne-de-proprete-dans-les-quartiers-sensibles/

## 4. Safety and insurance
- The employer must protect workers' health and safety (Labour Code arts. 95–96: https://lex.cameroun.cc/fr/cm/code-du-travail-1992/art-96).
- Arrêté n° 039/MTPS/IMT du 26 novembre 1984, art. 4, requires the employer to **provide and maintain personal protective equipment**: masks, gloves, protective clothing, etc. Source: https://legecam.cm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/bb-dcs-02-arrete-1984-mesures-dhygiene-et-de-securite.pdf
- **Kit per worker:** heavy-duty gloves, dust mask, high-visibility vest, closed shoes (check before the shift), and water.
- **Kit per crew:** first-aid kit, sharps container, waste bags, brooms/rakes. Buy the kit once and reuse it. The cost needs quotes; none is assumed here.
- **Rules:** a short safety briefing before every shift (to be added as a supervisor checklist item in the production app). No handling of medical or chemical waste: report it to the council instead. Work only on pavements and gutters, never in traffic.
- **Insurance:** CNPS work-accident cover (above). Also get a quote for third-party liability insurance from a local insurer.

## 5. Funding (no figures assumed)
- **Councils:** a service agreement with a commune under its art. 157 competence. The cost is small next to existing cleaning budgets (see the tender above).
- **NGOs and development programmes** for youth employment and sanitation.
- **Corporate sponsors (CSR):** a sponsor funds a zone or a month, with its logo on the vests (with the council's agreement).
- **Diaspora:** "sponsor a Saturday in your home town" (50,000 FCFA funds one week at the defaults), with photo reports from the app.
- Publish a simple monthly statement (money in, payouts, fees, kit) generated from the payout ledger.

## 6. Payout costs
- The demo simulates payouts. Production uses a payout aggregator (CamPay or Notch Pay) called from the server.
- **CamPay** lists a fee of **1.5 % per withdrawal (payout)** and 2 % per deposit: https://www.campay.net/fr/
  - 1.5 % of 5,000 FCFA = **75 FCFA per worker**, i.e. **≈ 750 FCFA/week** for 10 workers.
  - By default the app shows fees on top of the 50,000 FCFA. Admins can switch on "include fees in budget", which drops the crew to 9.
- Operator tariffs: MTN https://mtn.cm/fr/tarifs-momo/ · Orange https://www.orange.cm/fr/tarification-orange-money-update.html (found by search, not verified line by line).

## 7. Anti-fraud and fairness
- **Fair selection:** first come, first served per city. After a shift (or a no-show after confirming), the person goes to the back of the queue. One shift per person per week. People can see their queue position.
- **Identity:** one account per phone number (OTP sign-in in production). The payout goes only to the phone number registered at sign-up. An optional ID number is checked by the supervisor at the first shift.
- **Presence:** check-in needs both the worker's **one-time 6-digit code / QR** and **GPS within 300 m** of the meeting point (configurable). The production app also records check-out the same way. Before/after photos are required before a shift can be completed.
- **Payment controls:** a payout is created only on supervisor sign-off and paid only after admin approval. Each payout has an idempotency reference, so it can never be paid twice. The ledger is double-entry. Payouts are capped by the weekly budget in the database, not only in the UI.
- **Separation of duties:** supervisors cannot approve payouts, and admins cannot sign off attendance. All actions go to an audit log.
- **Spot checks:** an admin calls a random worker after each Saturday. Suspend accounts or supervisors on proven fraud.

## 8. Accounts and steps needed before a real launch
1. Supabase project (database, auth, edge functions) and an SMS OTP provider.
2. CamPay and/or Notch Pay merchant account **with payouts enabled** (business KYC). Keys go only into Supabase secrets.
3. Expo / EAS account, and a Google Play developer account (one-time $25) to publish the Android app.
4. Written authorization from the first commune, and a waste pick-up arrangement with its collection operator.
5. CNPS employer registration and a casual-labour declaration to the labour inspector.
6. PPE kit and insurance quotes.
7. Lawyer review of this plan, the per-shift engagement, and the privacy notice.
